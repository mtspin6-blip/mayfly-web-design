each note short and sourced. Tag every claim REAL / DERIVED / MODELED and give a source.

- `topics/<topic>.md`: weather triggers, habitat use, elevation and corridors, model results and accuracy.

## Seed facts (from the 2026-11-27 chat session)
- REAL (USGS 2018–2020, ND/SD juveniles): first fall migration median ~838 km, mean heading ~158° (SSE); migration
  moves > 310 km (n=147, median 684 km); regional moves 25–310 km (n=415, median 56 km). Pearse et al. 2023: migration
  distance rose ~166 km per 1 °C drop in minimum temperature; winter distribution differed a lot between years.
- MODELED (eBird S&T v2025, Sep 26–Jan 19 mean): top-10 Montana mallard cells: north Flathead valley
  (#1), Bighorn River near Hardin (#2), Fort Peck / C.M. Russell NWR area (4 of 10), Medicine Lake, Bowdoin, Gallatin
  valley near Three Forks, Kalispell area. Great Falls area is not in the statewide top 10 but shows up when zoomed in.
  Full list with coordinates: `outputs/report.md`.
- REAL (blue-winged teal, NOT mallard): 42 birds tagged SK/AB 2013, TX/LA 2015; 3 had fixes in MT, mostly the NE corner.
