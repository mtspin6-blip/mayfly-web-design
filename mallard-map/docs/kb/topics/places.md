# Places used to name hot spots (REF, approx)

Points are approximate centers (good to a few km), entered from general knowledge and refuge pages, **not surveyed**.
They only label hot spots ("nearest public area") and show as green/blue dots on the map. Before using one for access,
check FWP / USFWS maps. Source list: `PLACES` in `scripts/build_app.py`.

- NWRs: Benton Lake, Bowdoin, Medicine Lake, Red Rock Lakes, Lee Metcalf, Ninepipe, Pablo, UL Bend, Lake Mason,
  Hailstone (location least certain), Swan River, Lost Trail.
- WMAs/WPAs: Freezout Lake WMA (FWP spelling "Freezout"), Flathead WPA / North Shore WMA (S7: ~7 mi of north-shore
  shoreline; closed Mar 1–Jul 15, open in fall), Canyon Ferry WMA, Warm Springs WMA.
- Duck water: Fort Peck dredge cuts, Nelson, Fresno and Tiber reservoirs, Bighorn River (Fort Smith–Hardin), Missouri
  Headwaters, Lake Helena, Ennis Lake, Clark Canyon Reservoir, Missouri River at Great Falls.
- Fixed 2026-11-28: the old list called the #1 statewide cell "Swan River NWR (15 km)". It is the north shore of
  Flathead Lake (Flathead WPA / North Shore WMA, ~8 mi from Bigfork).
- Fixed 2026-11-28: Natural Earth labels the Missouri from Three Forks to Canyon Ferry as "Madison"; the build renames it Missouri north of 45.93°N.
