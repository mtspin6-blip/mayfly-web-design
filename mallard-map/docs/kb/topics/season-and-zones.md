# Season and zones (REG)

## Flyways and zones — **verified** against FWP regulation text as quoted in search results (S4)
- **Pacific Flyway:** Hill, Chouteau, Cascade, Meagher and Park counties and all counties west of them.
- **Central Flyway:** Blaine, Fergus, Judith Basin, Wheatland, Sweet Grass, Stillwater, Carbon and all counties east.
  - **Zone 2:** Big Horn, Carbon, Custer, Prairie, Rosebud, Treasure, Yellowstone. **Zone 1:** every other Central county.
- FWP proposed moving Sweet Grass and Stillwater to Zone 2; not confirmed adopted. **Check the 2026 PDF map.**
- The map draws zones from US Census county outlines (S13), dissolved by these lists.
